Until now, I’ve kept LinguAI working as a local-first app. The conversations and the feedback loop live on my device, and that made it easy to build something I could actually use.

The next step is moving into a real backend powered by AI. I’m planning to bring in AI agents, connect them with LangGraph, and use retrieval to fetch the most relevant context for each interaction. It feels like moving from “generation as an endpoint” toward something more intelligent and connected to real information, not just an API.
